About Us

Our Story

Welcome to Shree Beauty Parlour, where beauty meets tradition. Founded in 2010 by Mrs. Priya Sharma, we have been dedicated to providing exceptional beauty services that blend traditional Indian beauty practices with modern techniques.

Our journey began with a simple vision: to create a space where every woman could feel beautiful and confident. Over the years, we have grown into one of Mumbai's most trusted beauty destinations, known for our expertise in bridal makeup, mehendi, and comprehensive beauty treatments.

At Shree Beauty Parlour, we believe in the power of natural beauty enhanced by expert care. Our team of skilled professionals is committed to providing personalized services that bring out your inner radiance.
